Release 4.6
===========

This release improves Zig backend: it adds support for the `labeled continue optimization <https://github.com/ziglang/zig/issues/8220>`_,
thanks to `Pong <https://github.com/PongPong>`_ who contributed the implementation.

See the changelog: :ref:`releases/changelog/changelog:4.6 (2026-08-29)`.
